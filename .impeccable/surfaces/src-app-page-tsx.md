## Direction contract

THESIS: The semester is a ticket wallet. Every deadline is a coupon (a leg); the wallet always shows what remains, and finished legs are stamped USED instead of vanishing. Refuses the slate-sidebar card dashboard with stat tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Carrier navy #0D1B3D shell bars and ruled hairlines, jet red #D7262D for urgency and primary actions, coupon white on stock #F2F2F0, carbon purple #4B2E83 for every filled figure (dates, D-counts, marks), carbon-copy lilac panels. Condensed airline grotesk caps (Barlow Condensed) for heads and tabs, Barlow body, JetBrains Mono carbon numerals. Tab-cap labels, ruled fare boxes, dashed tear lines with punched notches, rotated VOID/USED stamps, `///` section marks, security-dot texture only in empty states.

STORY: A classmate opens the portal, sees the next coupon (course, item, date, time, marks, D-count) and the remaining legs; one tap opens a course's coupon book, assessments, calendar, notes, or resources. They trust it because sync time and who prepped/noted are printed on the stub.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: navy top bar "WINTER 2026 · IPE" + search; the next-leg coupon dominates (course caps 26px, item, DATE/TIME/MARKS ruled boxes, tear line, D-count stub 34px mono); "Remaining legs · n" list of compact coupons with D-count stubs; bottom navy tab bar (Today, Courses, Assess, Calendar, More). Desktop: navy left rail, next coupon + carbon-copy panel side by side, legs list and live-status table below.

FORM: Jet-age airline ticket wallet (catalog challenger vernacular-ephemera-jet-age-ticket-wallet, won by user choice over assigned position 6). seed:089670a24c40347b. Signature move: deadlines as tear-off coupons with D-count stubs; Done = rotated USED stamp. Motion: the stamp thunks in once; tear-off on first load only; reduced-motion holds still.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
